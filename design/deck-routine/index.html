Fly in, cut, shuffle, fly back

Your sequence: the deck starts face-up at its spot, flies to the centre while turning over and growing (as if it came closer to you), is cut three times, splits into two piles, and cards leave the two piles one by one to a third pile — the shuffle — the three piles forming a triangle. Then the third pile, now the whole deck, flies back and shrinks to its original size. Every card keeps its own edge strip wherever it goes.

Timing (about 6.9 s): fly-in 0.9 s · three cuts × 0.52 s · split 0.5 s · shuffle ≈ 2.6 s (a card leaves every 45 ms and takes 0.32 s to land) · fly-back 0.8 s. Motion: every move is minimum-jerk — speed and acceleration are zero at both ends — the same model as the flip. Cut sizes (35–65% of the deck) and the shuffle's runs (1–3 cards from alternating piles) are new each play. My assumption to confirm: the third pile sits at the apex above the two piles, toward the deck's spot, and the deck ends face-down.
