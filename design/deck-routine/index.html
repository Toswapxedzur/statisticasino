Bluffing Valley · deal animation · the dealer's routine

Fly in, middle cut, shuffle, fly to the deck spot

The real-game layout: the face-down deck lives top-left and the face-up used pile top-right, both just below the top bar. The used pile flies to the centre, turning over and growing. Then three middle-section cuts: a random middle section slides out horizontally, makes a flat U-turn 1.5 card-widths out (a 50% gap), and slides back horizontally onto the top. The top section falls onto the bottom and lands at the exact moment the middle's left edge touches the deck's right edge. Then the deck splits into two piles, cards go one by one to a third pile (the triangle shuffle), and the deck flies to the top-left and shrinks.

Timing (about 7.8 s): fly-in 0.9 s · three middle cuts × 0.76 s · split 0.5 s · shuffle ≈ 2.6 s (a card leaves every 45 ms and takes 0.32 s to land) · fly to the deck spot 0.8 s. Slow it to ¼× to see the landing and the edge-touch coincide. Motion: every move is minimum-jerk — speed and acceleration are zero at both ends — the same model as the flip. The middle sections (a random 25–40% of the deck, starting 20–40% down) and the shuffle's runs (1–3 cards from alternating piles) are new each play. Middle cut: the middle section follows one smooth motion over its whole path (straight out, U-turn, straight in), so its speed never jumps between segments. The top section only starts falling once the middle has fully cleared it, falls from rest like a drop, and lands exactly on the edge-touch — checked in the tests, along with no packet ever passing through another.
