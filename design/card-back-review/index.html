Every open-source card back we can use

86 backs from 11 sources, plus our old back for reference. Each is shown large and at in-game size (opponents' cards on a full table), where fine detail turns to texture. Grouped by licence, most permissive first; the site is public, so the licence matters. The vector backs (all Kennard, Nicu, BdRGames, Commons SVGs, Bellot) can be recoloured to our navy and gold.
