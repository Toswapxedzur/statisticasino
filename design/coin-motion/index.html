Bluffing Valley · currency animation · demo

Bets stream in, the pot sweeps up, the winner collects

A bet leaves the badge one coin column at a time (highest value first) and stacks onto a pile in front of the seat; a raise adds to it, a check moves nothing. When the street ends, every pile slides into the pot together, its number riding along and merging into the pot's number, which counts up. An uncalled bet slides back. The pot flies to the winner (a split pot: one share each), sinks into the badge, and the stack counts up.

Timing: a bet's columns leave 40 ms apart and land 0.38 s later · the sweep waits for the last bet to land (+0.15 s) and takes 0.52 s, all piles landing at the same instant · an uncalled bet slides home in 0.46 s · the win flies 0.6 s, sinks 0.22 s, and the number counts up over 0.52 s. The actions come faster here than at a real table, so the whole hand fits in about 15 s. Motion: every move is minimum-jerk, like the cards: speed and acceleration are zero at both ends. Coins lift slightly in flight (16% of the distance, 60 px at most), with no bounce. The piles are the table's own coin piles (60° zig-zag), in the table's real coin sizes. Sound: the game's own sounds, each played when coins land: a chip push per bet, one pot sound per sweep, the win sound when the winnings land, and the check knock on a check. A street where everyone checks makes no chip sound at all.
