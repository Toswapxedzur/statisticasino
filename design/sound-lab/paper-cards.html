Paper card sounds

The pack's paper cards recordings, as the 15 clips they were split into. Each clip is a quick burst of 2–14 taps. The waveform marks every tap, and each tap is also cut out on its own, so you can hear single taps. Where a clip became one of the game's current sounds, it says so.

Measured, not listened to. A "tap" is a sudden rise in loudness. For each tap you get: when it starts in the clip; its loudness, with 0 dB the loudest tap in that clip; how long after its start it reaches full loudness (≤ 20 ms is a crisp tap, more means it builds up and will sound late if used for a landing); and its brightness, where higher is thinner and snappier and lower is duller and weightier. Tap chips: ● a crisp tap · ○ one that builds up (> 30 ms to full loudness).
