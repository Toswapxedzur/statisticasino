Generic click sounds

Every click-type sound in our licensed library (160 single clicks from four sources), each shown with its own library label. Long takes with several clicks, like "pen click" or "industrial switch", are cut into their single clicks. Every clip starts on its hit. Press a tile to hear it.

Clean = no pitch, one single hit, and that hit within 12 ms of the start, so it lands exactly on an animation. These come first in each library. Each tile shows: length (how long the click rings before dying away; short is tight, long is heavier) and brightness (higher is thinner and snappier, lower is duller and weightier). Everything that failed a check is under "Also in this library", with the reason: rings (pitched), two hits, or the hit comes late.
