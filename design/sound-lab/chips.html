Chip sounds by material

The chip recordings we already have, laid out as amount (rows: one chip, a few, a pile, all-in) by material (columns). Ceramic and clay are ArtOrDie's two chip recordings, from the same pack as the paper cards; other poker chips are Kenney's casino chips and Freesound recordings; metal coins are last round's style, for comparison. Up to three per cell, one per source recording, and no sound appears twice. Press a tile to hear it.

How they were chosen: every recording was split into single sound events. ArtOrDie's clips are bursts of several clicks, so their single clicks were also cut out. Each event was measured, filtered by hit count for its amount (1 · 2–4 · 5+ · a long 6+ push for all-in) and for at most a light ring, then ranked by CLAP against descriptions of poker chips (or coins, for the metal column). CLAP pre-sorts; your ears decide. Empty cells: the short ArtOrDie clips don't contain a long push, so there's no ceramic all-in and only one clay one.
