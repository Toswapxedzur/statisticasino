Bluffing Valley modifications: individual glyphs/figures were extracted from the full
cards and recomposed (resized/repositioned) into the app's own card layout. These
modified SVG sources are distributed here in full, as required by the LGPL.

The corner-index layout and the pip arrangement are original
Bluffing Valley work.
