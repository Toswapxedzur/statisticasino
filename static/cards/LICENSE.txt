Modification by Riverside: the corner rank+suit indices have been scaled up
(~1.5x, anchored at each corner) for on-screen legibility. All other art —
pips, suit shapes, and the court (J/Q/K) figures — is unchanged from the
original. As required by the LGPL, these modified SVG sources are distributed
here in full (each *.svg is the editable source).

The card back and empty-slot placeholder are original Riverside artwork, not
part of Aguilar's deck.
